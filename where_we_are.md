# Extremely Tentative Conclusions
To provide a summary of where this investigation currently is, a basic outline will be provided below. This outline is not a conclusion of fact, but a record of where the focus of the investigation currently leans.


### Shooter Location:
The investigation is not focusing on the Losee Center as the shooting location, on Tyler Robinson as a primary suspect, or on the figure seen on the Losee Center at the time of the crime (whoever that figure may or may not be.) That is because the rifle recovered associated with that firing position does not match the wound that was observed nor the information we have about the internal wound from the surgeon. However, it is believed that a rifle—of unknown caliber—was indeed fired from that position at the exact moment the crime took place. It is however not the focus of this investigation because the damage done to the victim is inconsistent with the damage expected of that weapon and shooting location.

Most shooting locations within the crowd, in the tent victim sat under, and on other rooftops are also not the subject of primary focus. That is primarily due to the high visibility of those areas, lack of audience attestation both verbal and physical, and lack of observable recoil of suspected firearm devices in those areas.

The focus of this investigation is currently on a shooting location within a van in the tunnel behind victim. That is because a shooter in that location can remain practically invisible with blankets for the entirety of their time on scene, because many firearm types and accessories could be chosen without increasing visual footprint, and because recoil could be made invisible with blankets. However, the practical plausibility of this shooting position is not evidence it was used; it is simply receiving greater focus at this stage than other possible locations.
