# Drone Shooter Theory
The shot came from a military-grade drone likely deployed from the ground. Guidance was provided by an infrared (IR) laser courtesy of an individual near victim at the time.

### Evidence in Support:
- A smudge of light coming from an iPhone screen
- Such technology appears to exist as early as 2015

### Analysis of Evidence:
It is highly likely the smudge of light coming from the phone's screen is utterly inocuous, as most smudges of light are.

### Opposing Evidence:
- A laser source would emit a bright, tiny point of light, not a wide smudge.
- No sound/visual evidence of a drone in the area capable of causing cavitation.
- If the drone was far away, it must fire a supersonic round, but no second supersonic crack is heard.

### Conclusion:
There is no reliable evidence that an IR laser or firearm-equipped drone was used. However, it cannot be ruled out.
